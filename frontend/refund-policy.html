PAYMENT AND REFUNDS

Refund policy

Refunds are reviewed against the order and the return or cancellation outcome.

Refund eligibility

A refund may be due when an order is cancelled before fulfilment, a return is approved, an item is defective/wrong/not as described, or another refund is required under applicable law. The shop will confirm the decision and amount with you.

How to request a refund

Use the Contact page, choose “Return or refund”, and provide your order number and checkout phone number. Include the reason and any supporting photos or details. Do not send card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords, or one-time codes.

Refund method and timing

  • Cash on delivery: after approval, we will contact you to arrange a suitable bank or UPI transfer and confirm the required details securely. We will never ask for your UPI PIN.
  • UPI: this shop currently records UPI orders for manual verification; it does not automatically verify payment or initiate a gateway refund. Contact us with the order reference and we will confirm the refund arrangement.
  • Processing time depends on verification and the receiving bank/payment provider. We will communicate the expected timing when the refund is approved.

Order cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible to request cancellation. If an order has already been packed or handed to a delivery provider, cancellation may require a return after delivery. This does not affect statutory rights.

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Refund workflow must be updated if a payment gateway or courier provider is added.
