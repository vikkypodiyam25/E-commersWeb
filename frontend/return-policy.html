SHOPPING WITH CONFIDENCE

Return policy

If something is wrong with an order, tell us and we will review it with you.

When to contact us

Contact us if an item arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, materially different from its listing, or if you have another issue with the order. Please report the issue as soon as reasonably possible after delivery so we can investigate; reporting promptly helps, but this request does not remove any rights available under applicable consumer law.

How to request a return

  1. Open the Contact page and select “Return or refund”.
  2. Provide your order number, checkout phone number, the item involved, and a short description of the issue.
  3. For damage or an incorrect item, include clear photos if you can.
  4. Wait for the shop to confirm the next step and collection/return arrangements before sending an item back.

Condition and review

Keep the item and its packaging, accessories, and proof of purchase while we review your request. We may inspect the item to understand the issue. We will explain the outcome and any applicable return arrangement directly to you.

Exceptions and consumer rights

Some items may have specific return conditions shown on their product listing. Nothing in this policy is intended to limit rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. If goods are defective, not as described, or otherwise qualify for a remedy under applicable law, contact us so we can address the issue.

Orders are confirmed by the shop after checkout. If delivery is delayed beyond the represented timeframe, contact us; any statutory rights remain unaffected.
