YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy policy

This page explains what information this shop collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Order details you submit: name, phone number, delivery address, selected payment method, items and total.
  • Messages you send through the Contact page, including your name, phone number, optional email, topic and message.
  • A random, HttpOnly cart cookie used to keep your shopping bag associated with your browser.
  • Saved-product preferences are stored in your browser’s local storage.

Why we use it

We use the information to maintain your cart, process and confirm orders, check order status, respond to support requests, and prevent misuse of the shop.

Where information is stored

Products, carts, and contact requests are stored in the shop’s local SQLite database. Orders are stored in MongoDB only when the shop operator has configured a MongoDB connection; otherwise they are stored in the local SQLite database. The operator hosting this site controls access to those systems.

Sharing and payments

We do not sell customer details. We may share the minimum details needed with a delivery or payment provider if one is engaged to fulfil your order. This version does not have an integrated payment gateway or courier connection. UPI payments, when configured, are manually verified.

Cookies, security, and retention

The cart cookie is necessary for the bag to work. Admin session cookies are used only for shop management. We use reasonable access controls, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Order and contact records are retained in the shop database until the shop operator removes them or a legal obligation requires retention.

Your choices and requests

You can clear saved products through your browser storage. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information, contact the shop and include enough information to identify the relevant order. Requests may be subject to record-keeping requirements and applicable law.

Children and policy changes

This shop is intended for general customers and is not designed to collect information from children independently. We may update this policy when the shop’s practices change; the date above reflects the latest edit.

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Operator should update this policy when storage, analytics, payment, or delivery integrations change.
