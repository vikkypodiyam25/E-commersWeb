About My Shop VIP

Good everyday tech and helpful service, brought closer to home in Dantewada.

Our story

My Shop VIP is a small online storefront for everyday electronics and accessories. We are based around Metapal, Dantewada, and want to make browsing and ordering simple for local customers.

How shopping works

  • Browse the product catalog and check the listed price and available stock.
  • Add items to your bag and submit your delivery details at checkout.
  • We receive the order and contact you to confirm the order and delivery arrangements.
  • Cash on delivery is available. UPI is shown only when shop payment details have been configured; UPI payments are manually verified.
Product listings currently include starter/demo catalog entries. Please confirm product availability and details with the shop before relying on a listing.

Questions or feedback?

Use our Contact page for order questions, product queries, or feedback. For an existing purchase, include your order number and the phone number used at checkout.
